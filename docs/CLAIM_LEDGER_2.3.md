| Claim | Status | Evidence | Non-claim / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| H0: caching and ordinary decision theory explain transfer effects | Not rejected | Earlier baselines and efficiency accounting | No novel physical law implied |
| H1: nonadditive procedure utility is relevant | Synthetic-support only | Planted high-order interactions, Möbius differences | No natural autonomous causal synergy demonstrated |
| H2: genuine critical crystallization threshold | **Unestablished** | No finite-size criticality evidence | Stable controller ≠ phase transition |
| H3: general self-sustaining learning-to-learn | **Unestablished** | Bounded synthetic retention and reuse | External tasks, honest evidence, costs still required |
| H4: independent channels detect isolated deception | **Supported conditionally (synthetic)** | 1,904 alarms and 0 incorrect *fresh* guard certifications in each isolated-corruption holdout; replicated | Needs independence/provenance assumptions and incurs HOLD/cost |
| Common-mode deception defeat | **Observed synthetic counterexample** | 600 incorrect fresh guard certifications, 0 alarms; replicated at 289 | Agreement cannot prove ground truth |
| Stale reuse can still be harmful | **Observed** | 16 harmful guard deployments in each isolated-corruption holdout | Fresh certification success does not establish safe deployment |
| Guard is uniformly superior | **Refuted** | Primary/anchor periodic outperform guard in multiple worlds | Cost and false alarms matter |
| Pegasus application | **Not tested** | No live agent runs | No authorization or production safety claim |

Statistical guarantee: simultaneous bounded-sample concentration for declared honest current-epoch channels, fixed finite horizon and valid sampling assumptions. It is not a verification of the assumptions, not a future-environment guarantee, and cannot detect correlated bias by itself.
