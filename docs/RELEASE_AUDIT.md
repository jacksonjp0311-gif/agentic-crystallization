## Mathematics and API

See `SOURCE_AUDIT.md`: Hoeffding union coverage requires a fixed catalog/horizon/sample
design and suitable within-stream observations. Cross-channel independence is not needed
for the union bound, and agreement does not prove honesty. Optional within-stream stopping
is not covered by the fixed-horizon theorem. Uncertain costs require additional error bounds.
No anytime-valid or future-stationarity guarantee is added.

The independent API validates two candidates, baseline/context, bounded paired samples,
prespecified counts, provenance declarations, audit ordering, cost and expiry.
It emits CERTIFY_CURRENT/HOLD/STALE/ALARM with receipts and local duplicate protection.
It cannot authenticate a producer's truth, sampling declarations or timestamps.
Confidence composition across distinct protocols remains caller responsibility.

## Security and outstanding gates

The tracked-text and extracted-PDF pattern scan found no credential or personal-path
findings. One scanner-regex self-match is explicitly allowlisted.
Pattern scanning is not a proof of secret absence. Image contents and nested historical
archive contents need independent public-disclosure review before public release.
No private Pegasus receipts, transcripts or RecallTree memory were copied.
No license has been selected. Branch-protection recommendations are in SECURITY.md;
protection enforcement has not been configured.
No historical version tags are fabricated from the modern tree; original archives retain
their own historical identities and recorded SHA256 hashes.
