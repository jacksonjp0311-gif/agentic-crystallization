# Agentic Crystallization v1.9 — Context packet for research agents

> **Authoritative scope:** A standalone mathematical/synthetic research package. No instruction here grants access to Pegasus, self-modification authority, or deployment permission.

## Research intent

Investigate whether interacting, validated procedure knowledge can later organize into a useful and stable adaptive selection mechanism. v1.9 is strictly the **evaluation layer**: reducing evidence costs for selecting among a frozen candidate set with provable conditional risk control.

## Precise current state

- **H1:** Standard finite-difference/Möbius interaction values exist; planted synthetic nonadditivity observed in v1.0–1.1. No universal phase physics.
- **H2:** Critical graph-mediated self-organization transition **not established**.
- **H3:** Repeated autonomous improvement and lower future adaptation cost **not established**.
- **v1.8:** Fixed sample statistical certificates conditional on known shift bound. Historical failure under shift violation.
- **v1.9:** Finite-checkpoint and unbounded-checkpoint union-bound confidence rules; synthetic evidence savings under valid shift, with severe failures under false shift.

**Core theorem:** Fixed action set K, iid task vectors, f_a∈[0,1], fixed known costs, valid joint-TV bound δ, predeclared looks n_j. With r_j=sqrt(log(2 K J/α)/(2 n_j)), target utility lies simultaneously in clipped mean±(r_j+δ)-cost intervals with probability ≥1−α. `min lower(selected) > max upper(other)` ensures target-domain strict best selection on this event. This does not prove any unknown shift bound.
